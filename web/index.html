Cards & trust

This is for card holders. Sign in with the code on your access card to issue cards, revoke them, and see who the board thinks has earned moderation.

Issue a card

Hand the printed card over in person. Whoever holds it is that person, so treat it like a key — and revoke it the moment it goes missing.

Permission level

Issued cards

Codes are never shown again — only their hashes are kept.

Earned trust

Who the board thinks is worth moderation, scored on countable behaviour. Any message of theirs that has been flagged rules them out entirely. Automation can grant a community moderator card and nothing higher — a moderator cannot issue cards, so the bot can never manufacture an official.
